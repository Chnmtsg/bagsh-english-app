# Хуваарь — the 84-day plan, on the clock

Companion to the Study Plan operational layer. Site shift 07:00–19:00 with
1–2 free hours inside it; home blocks alternate. Three tools, three jobs:

| Tool | Job | Never |
|---|---|---|
| **Small Step** | Reviews, lesson drills, your own errors, production checks, the tracking numbers | Vocabulary chunk cards |
| **Anki** | Chunk cards from your reading, 8–12/day | Grammar or error items — one scheduler per fact |
| **QQ English** | Live conversation + a written error list every session | Grammar lessons — that is what the drills are for |

## Site day — 60 min baseline, 120 when the shift allows

| Clock | Min | Strand | Do | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 06:20–06:35 | 15 | Language | Reviews only. Хичээллэх, whatever is due. Never new material here. | Small Step › Зам |
| Free hour 1 (any time 09–16) | 20 | Input | Listening at 95% comprehension. Odd days non-technical. | Podcast |
| Free hour 1 | 20 | Language | Today's lesson: read the page, then drill the unit. | Small Step › Унших → Дасгал |
| Free hour 2 or break | 10 | Output | 10 sentences about today's work, typed. Check. | Small Step › Бичих |
| 19:25–19:40 | 15 | Language | Your errors due today + new items. Anki reviews. | Small Step › Зам, Anki |
| 21:45–21:55 | 10 | Fluency | Shadow the same 30-second clip 5–10×. | Phone |

**When you have two free hours (120-min day):** add 35 min output in free hour 2
(write, or on days 3/7/10/14 record 3 min → transcribe → paste into Бичих →
Шалгуулах), 15 min reading in the evening building 12 Anki cards, and 5 min
4/3/2 after shadowing. Lessons do not speed up — the extra hour is output.

## The four numbers, every day 14

Words · errors/100w · ART/100w · clauses/sentence — from the recording's
check on Бичих. Errors down and clauses up is the signal. Both flat after
two blocks means more QQ sessions, not more study.

## Bad day

10 minutes of reviews. Never skip two days running. Halve every slot rather
than dropping a strand.
